Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your email address (only when you make a purchase, to deliver your ebook), and anonymous page-view data — no email or personal identifier attached to that.

Why we collect it (consent)

We only collect your email because you choose to give it to us at checkout, to deliver your purchase. By completing a purchase, you consent to us using your email for that purpose and for order-related communication.

Who can see your email

Only the site administrator. Purchase emails are shown in the admin dashboard for 30 days after purchase, then hidden from that view. The underlying record is kept longer, as required for accounting and tax purposes, but isn't displayed in the day-to-day admin view after that window.

How long we keep it (retention)

Order records, including your email, are retained for up to 8 years to meet accounting and tax record-keeping requirements. Anonymous page-view data is not tied to your identity and is retained for general site analytics only.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your email with advertisers or third parties.

Cookies

We use a minimal flag to remember you've seen the cookie notice, and anonymous analytics to understand site traffic.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent for future use, at any time by contacting us below. We'll respond to reasonable requests without undue delay.

Grievance Officer

For any privacy complaint or data request, contact our Grievance Officer: p.parthasarathi7580@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge complaints within a reasonable timeframe.

This policy is written to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023, as its rules are phased in through 2027. This is a starting template, not legal advice — have it reviewed by a professional before relying on it for a real store, especially as DPDP enforcement rules continue to roll out.
