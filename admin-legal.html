How to write (tap to open)
## Heading makes a section heading.
- item makes a bullet point.
**bold** makes bold text.
[text](https://example.com) makes a link. You can also use mailto:you@mail.com or a page like contact.html.
A blank line starts a new paragraph.

Anything inside [[ double square brackets ]] is a blank you still have to fill in. A page that still has one is not shown to visitors: they see "This page is being updated".
You cannot add HTML or scripts here. They would only show as plain text.
