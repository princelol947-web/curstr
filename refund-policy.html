Refund Policy

1. Digital goods

Every item sold here is a digital ebook, delivered instantly by download link after payment. Because the file is available to you immediately, refund rules for digital goods work differently than for physical products.

2. General policy

Once your download link has been sent, the sale is generally final. This is standard for instant-delivery digital products — it protects against a file being downloaded and then refunded afterward.

✓ 3. When you ARE eligible for a refund

We'll refund you in full if any of these happen:

  • The file is corrupted, incomplete, or won't open
  • You received the wrong title
  • You were charged more than once for the same order
  • You were charged but never received a download link at all

✕ 4. When you're NOT eligible for a refund

We generally can't offer a refund if:

  • You changed your mind after downloading the file
  • You entered the wrong email address at checkout (double-check before paying)
  • More than 7 days have passed since purchase

5. How to request a refund

Contact us within 7 days of purchase with your order confirmation and the issue you ran into. Refunds are typically reviewed within 2–3 business days, and approved refunds are returned to your original payment method — banks typically take 5–10 business days to show the funds.

6. Chargebacks

If you're unhappy with a purchase, please contact us directly first — we'd rather fix it than have you file a chargeback with your bank, which can take longer and adds fees on both sides.

This is a starting template, not legal advice. Some regions (including parts of the EU and UK) legally require a cooling-off period even for digital goods unless the buyer explicitly waives it at checkout — check your local consumer-protection law before launch, and adjust the 7-day window above if needed.
